Why Now

The market found us before we built the technology.

More than fifty thousand people arrive at Renters.com every month without a single paid ad, drawn by a brand that clearly signals what they are looking for. That organic reach is a foundation most early-stage platforms spend years and significant capital trying to build.

The constraint is the other side of the marketplace. We have far more people looking for homes than homes to show them, and closing that gap is where the work is. It is also why the supply side is free to join, free to list, and priced only on results.

We are building the matching and verification technology that converts that traffic into successful placements.

The Problem

Search is the wrong interface for renting.

Every incumbent monetizes listing volume, so every incumbent shows you as many results as possible. The renter does the filtering, the qualifying and the chasing, and pays application fees discovering what they were never eligible for. The landlord fields the resulting inbound and screens it by hand.

For Renters

Weeks of chasing listings that are already gone, and application fees spent on places they never qualified for, because requirements are not disclosed until after they apply.

Time and money lost on every search.

For Landlords

Weeks of unqualified inquiries and no-show showings before finding a suitable tenant. The screening burden falls entirely on the landlord.

Longer vacancies. More work. Same result.

How It Works

Nothing is browsable. Everything is matched.

There is no listing search on Renters.com and no results page. A renter draws the areas they would genuinely live in and describes what would work there, by voice or by text. A landlord states rent, availability and the criteria a renter has to clear. We evaluate every new unit against every open request in both directions, and deliver only what passes.

Both sides confirm identity with a government ID and a live face match before they can transact. Every listing is reviewed by a person before it goes live.

✓  Why this is defensible

The incumbents cannot copy this. Their revenue is listing exposure, and a model that shows a renter four places instead of four hundred destroys the inventory they sell. Removing search is only rational for a platform paid on outcomes.

✓  The marketplace is free for renters

Stating a request, identity verification, matching and introductions all at no cost. Renters are never charged to be matched or to move in. The only renter-paid product is an optional concierge search, described below, which a renter buys deliberately or not at all.

Revenue Model

Nobody pays to be here. Everybody pays for what they get.

Listing is free on every plan and for every account type. Supply-side members choose between paying per verified lead OR paying a single flat fee on a confirmed move-in. No subscriptions, no listing fees, and no paid placement.

Per lead

$35

Charged for each identity-verified renter matched and delivered to a landlord, property manager or agent. Lower ticket, higher frequency, and it converts a listing into revenue long before a lease is signed.

On move-in

$650

Charged only when a renter we delivered confirms they have moved in. $150 of the fee is paid to that renter, which is what closes the attribution loop and gives us verified placement data.

The two plans break even at nineteen leads per filled unit, so the supply-side member picks based on their own conversion rate. Both are flat fees that do not scale with rent, which keeps us a marketing channel rather than a brokerage — a distinction that matters for licensure in every state we operate in.

+  Renter-side, optional — Concierge search

A renter short on time can hire us to run the search on their behalf by hand, including inventory outside our own network, and we book up to five confirmed showings. A flat $500, paid by the renter, entirely optional.

Strategically this is a supply acquisition engine. Running these searches ourselves has meant direct conversations with dozens of landlords per search who are not yet on the platform.

+  In development — Embedded financial products

Lease guarantee and co-signing, landlord protection, deposit coverage, and renters insurance, offered through licensed partners at the moment of highest intent, when a renter is qualifying for a home. Co-signing is the first to launch.

Each successful placement becomes a durable financial relationship, not a one-time transaction.
